Used to all kinds of records, the Game of Thrones series made HBO’s heyday from 2011 to 2019. However, since the end of the phenomenon, the pay channel has seen its audience drop massively on its adult target – from 18 to 49 years. According to a report by Variety, a decrease of 51% was recorded during the year 2020. While other quality programs, such as His Dark Materials, Lovecraft Country or The Undoing, continue to flourish on the screen, they only bring together a reduced target, called “niche”. Launched in May 2020, the HBO Max platform has, for its part, had a complicated start, even if the arrival of large productions – like Wonder Woman 1984, offered simultaneously in theaters and on the platform – should move things.
Other American channels are also affected by a considerable fall, such as Showtime, Starz, Starz Encore and Cinemax. A trend that underlines, once again, the stiff competition imposed by streaming platforms, very much in demand during periods of confinement throughout 2020.
The most tweeted series in history, the most awarded at the Emmy Awards in 2015 and the most downloaded – before being overtaken by The Mandalorian recently -, Game Of Thrones had an average of twelve million viewers for each episode. The very last, broadcast on May 19, 2019, exploded these figures to reach an audience of 19.3 million. A televisual as well as a cultural event. The arrival, in 2022, of the spin-off, entitled House of the Dragon, should restore color to the chain. The shooting of this new project is scheduled for the next few weeks, in the studios Leavesden in Watford, England.
