 KATHMANDU: The police have arrested five allegedly fake candidates in medical examinations.
 According to SSP Basanta Kunwar, the crime branch of the metropolitan police nabbed the fake examinees attending medical science exam on Friday.
 He informed that the arrested ones include four fake examinees and one student working as a middleman to arrange for the fake students.
 They are arrested from Baneshwor, Balkumari and Bhaktapur.
 The police have not made their details public yet.
