The Mumbai Traffic Police have issued an order putting traffic restrictions in place from 3 pm till midnight on Wednesday as well as Thursday, owing to large immersion processions of Devi idols across Mumbai concluding the nine days Navratri festival.
 As part of the restrictions, some portions of 22 roads will be closed for vehicular traffic, 18 roads will have one way traffic movement and 45 roads will have parking restrictions in Mumbai.
 The roads which will be closed for vehicular traffic include:
- In south Mumbai, some portions of Sardar Vallabhai Patel road, VP road, JSS road, Pandita Ramabai road and Dattaram Lad Road.
 - In central Mumbai, portions of Ranade Road, Veer Savarkar road, Keluskar road, MB raut road, SK Bole road, Takandas Kataria road, All Sant Rohidas road, all 90 and 60 feet roads, Mahim Sion Link road will be shut for vehicular traffic.
 - In eastern suburbs, some portions of the Dindayal Upadhyay road, Jangal mangal road and Tank road will be shut for vehicular traffic.
 - In western suburbs, some parts of K. T Soni road will be shut.
 Apart from road closures mentioned above, 18 roads will have one way traffic:
- These roads in south Mumbai are captain Prakash Pethe road, Rambhau Salgaonkar road, Nathalal Parekh road, Pandita Ramabai road, Kennedy bridge, Grand road bridge, Patthe Bapurao road, Javji Dadaji road, Bellasis bridge, French bridge and Kalbadevi road.
 - In Central Mumbai, there will be one way traffic on Veer Savarkar road, Bal Govind Das road, 60 feet road, T. H. Kataria road and in western suburbs one way traffic will be on KT Soni road and Bhandarpada road.
 Another 45 roads in the city will have parking restrictions :
- In south Mumbai parts of All Nathalal Parekh Marg, All Rambhau Salgaonkar Marg, Capt. Prakash Pethe Marg, Sardar Vallabhai Patel road, Dr Dadasaehb Badkamkar Marg, V. P Road, JSS Road, Pandita Ramabai Marg, Justice Sitaram Patkar Marg, Ausgust Kranti Marg, Javji Dadaji Marg, Walkeshwar Road, Nepean Sea Road, L. T Marg and B. J Marg will have parking restrictions.
 - In Central Mumbai parking restrictions will be in place portions of Rande Road, Veer Savarkar Marg, All Keluskar Road (North and South), All M. B. Raut Marg, S. K Bode Road, Dyneshwar Mandir Marg, Jambhekar Maharaj Marg, Mori Road, T. H Kataria Marg, T. H Kataria road, Dr Anni Besant Road, L. B. S Road, New Mill Road.
 - In central Mumbai these include Hemu Kalani Marg, Sion Panvel Mahamarg, All Ghatkopar Mankhurd Bridge, V. N Purav Marg and S. G Barve Marg.
 - Roads with parking restrictions in western suburbs include Juhu Tara Road, V. M Rd. , S. V Rd. , Damu Anna Date Marg, M. G Road, K. T Soni Road and Bandarpakhadi Rd.
