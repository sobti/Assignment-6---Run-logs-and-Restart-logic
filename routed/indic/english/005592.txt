HARARE: India has won the third One-day International match against Zimbabwe by 83 runs in Harare.
Zimbabwe won the toss and decided to field first. India set Zimbabwe 277 runs target to win the match. Indian batsman KM Jadhav scored 105 runs and remained not out and Pandey made 71 runs in the match. Zimbabwean bowler Madziva took two wickets. While Chibhabha, Masakadza and Utseya took one wicket each.
Zimbabwean team scrod 193 runs in 42.4 overs. Opener Chibhabha made 82 runs and Chakabva scored 27 runs. Indian bowler Binny grabbed three wickets while Sharma, Harbhajan Singh and Patel took two wickets each. Indian star Jadhav was awarded with Man of the Match.
