Comedian and model Sagar Lamsal 'Bale' has claimed that the news of a 'breakup' between him and model Prisma Khatiwada was false. He claimed in a program organized in Kathmandu on Sunday that they were just good friends.
 Sagar also said that they will start working together soon. 'I myself have been away from the media for a few months now. Due to my family problems, I have not been able to appear in the media,' he said. He was emotional remembering their past. I became a weeping prince, an apologetic prism, and an abusive person. '
Sagar said that even the news that his marriage was about to take place was not true. In the public program of the song 'Ho Ho Ni Maya' organized in Kathmandu, Sagar said, 'There are no plans to get married now. ' Sagar even said that Prisma and Princy were the same for him. He said, 'Prisma and Princy may be different for others, they are the same for me. Everyone has a lifelong friend, mine is Prisma and Princy. '
He said that he is preparing to make a series of songs 'Timro Man Badliyecha' and that the video will have Prisma and Princy.
