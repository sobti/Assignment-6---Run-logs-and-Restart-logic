**Bhubaneswar: ** Odisha CM Naveen Patnaik launched the online ticket sales for Hockey Men’s World Cup which is scheduled to be played at Kalinga Stadium in Bhubaneswar from 28 November to 16 December 2018. He received the first ticket of the tournament from Elena Norman, CEO of Hockey India in the presence of Chandra Sarathi Behera, Odisha Sports Minister.
“I am glad to announce the launch of the online ticket sales for the Odisha Hockey Men’s World Cup Bhubaneswar 2018. May the hockey fever of the people of Odisha spread to the world,” the CM said while launching the online ticket sales.
“I am delighted to have received the first ticket. We look forward to hosting fans and visitors from all over,” he added.

A total of 36 matches will be played during the tournament. The opening match will see World No. 3 Belgium take on World No. 11 Canada on November 28 while host India will also open their campaign on the same day when they take on South Africa.
