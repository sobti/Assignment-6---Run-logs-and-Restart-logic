Vineeth Sreenivasan is teaming up with actor Pranav Mohanlal for his next directorial titled 'Hridayam'. The movie's pre-production works are on full swing and the movie is expected to go on floors very soon. Recently, Vineeth Sreenivasan visited Istanbul to record live instruments for the film. Vineeth, who frequently updates his fans about his new movies took to his social media handle to share some photos.
"Recording live instruments with some incredibly talented musicians in Istanbul for Hridayam! past few days have been a great learning experience. Can’t wait to bring the songs out to you guys. keep us in prayers. ?," he captioned.
Bankrolled by Visakh Subramaniam under the banner Merryland Cinemas, and co-produced by Nobel Babu Thomas, 'Hridayam' will have Mollywood's favorite star kids Pranav Mohanlal and Kalyani Priyadarshan in the lead roles. ‘Mayaanadi’ fame Darshana Rajendran is also playing an important role in the movie. There are rumors are abuzz that Nivin Pauly will have an extended cameo in the movie.
Vineeth Sreenivasan has penned the script of the movie which will have music composed by Hesham Abdul Wahab.
