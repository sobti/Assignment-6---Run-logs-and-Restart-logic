The constant discussion among the movie buffs will mostly be Who is the current No. 1? and Who are the top 5 heroes in the Industry? . Cosmopolitan Magazine has come forward to clear these doubts of the audience.
 In a recent survey conducted by the popular magazine to figure out the Top 5 heroes in Telugu Film Industry, Pawan Kalyan once again emerged as the undisputed king of Tollywood outshining his competitor Mahesh Babu.
 Here is the Top 5 list:
1) Pawan Kalyan: Gabbar Singh changed the cards and he is a true example of a star.
 2) Mahesh Babu: Back on success track with Dookudu and hasn't lost the form ever since then.
 3) Ram Charan: Although Magadheera remains always special in his career, it was his recent hist 'Racha' and 'Nayak' showed he is here to stay for long.
 4) Jr NTR: Baadshah came as a big relief to Young Tiger, after average films like 'Oosaravelli' and 'Dammu'.
 5) Allu Arjun: 'Julayi' has put an end to his flop streak and his recent flick 'Iddarammayilatho' too did some good business at the ticket window.
