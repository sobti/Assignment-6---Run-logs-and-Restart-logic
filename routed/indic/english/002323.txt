The wise leaders of the Netherlands have decided not to slap a new tax on MP3 players and digital video recorders, agreeing with electronics manufacturers that such a levy would really stick it to consumers.
The Dutch government and other bodies had been considering extending a fee -- originally appended to audio tapes -- intended to compensate musicians and other artists to include digital devices.
But the Ministry of General Affairs sagely reasoned that since most of the content on such devices was legally acquired (Did someone say something funny, Mr. Wise Guy? All right, then.), consumers would essentially be paying twice for copyright fees.
