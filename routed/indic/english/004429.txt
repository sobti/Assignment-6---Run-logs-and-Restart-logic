Holidays and special occasions do bring people together! It is known that Jennifer Aniston and Justin Theroux, who amicably parted ways a few years ago, last year reunited during Thanksgiving celebration. The actress is celebrating her 51st birthday today and received a sweet message from her ex-husband Justin Theroux.
Justin took to his Instagram stories to share a black-and-white photograph of Aniston wearing a tank top and jeans. “. grabbing 2020 and another year just like —” Theroux wrote. “Happy birthday B,” he added with a red heart emoji.
Her Friends co-star also shared an adorable photo to mark the birthday. "No matter how hard you might try. there’s only one Jennifer Aniston. Happy birthday my dear friend @jenniferaniston! I love you!♥️♥️♥️," she wrote on Instagram.
Theroux and Aniston amicably parted ways in February 2018 after seven years of relationship and two and a half years of marriage.
