Tirupati, Nov 12: The famous TTD sub-shrine of Sri Kalyanavenkateswara Swamy at Narayanavanam is all set for the five-day Teppotsavam which commences from November 13 to November 17.
On first day the processional deity of Lord Sri Rama along with Goddess Sitadevi and Lakshmana Swamy will take a pleasure ride on the float in the finely decked temple tank. On the second day Goddess Andal will grace the devotees on float while the last three days, Lord Malayappa along with His two beloved consorts, Sridevi and Bhudevi bless the devotees. This float festival takes place between 5.30pm to 8pm every day.
