 During his visit to Nagpur, PM Modi will lay the foundation stone and inaugurate projects worth Rs 75,000 crore, according to a statement issued by the Prime Minister's Office (PMO) in New Delhi. Also, the Prime Minister will launch the Vande Bharat Express (between Nagpur and Bilaspur) and phase I of the Nagpur Metro.
 Sources said the construction is a part of the 'Metro Revolution' happening under the Narendra Modi government in the country. To note, in 2014, only five cities had a Metro network. Today, that figure stands at 20 cities. In 2014, the entire Metro network in the country was 248 km. Today, the total Metro network in the country is 775 km.
 India's first indigenous mRNA vaccine candidate has received approval from the Drugs Controller General of India to initiate Phase I/II human clinical trial.
 "The vaccine is proving to be safe through phase I clinical trials and that it is proving to produce a positive antibody response," Minister for Health Greg Hunt said.
 The vaccine is being evaluated in Phase-I clinical trials in India. The vaccine maker early this month received approval from the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation to conduct the Phase-II trials.
