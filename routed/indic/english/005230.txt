It appears that Jerry Jones and head coach Mike McCarthy saw enough. Not in their 19-12 divisional-round loss to the San Francisco 49ers, though that clearly played a part in it all. The Cowboys and the rest of the NFC sat and saw the Philaelphia Eagles solve Demeco Ryans’ 49ers defense on Sunday, scoring four rushing touchdowns to earn their second trip to the Super Bowl in the last six seasons.
That effort must have cemented the organizations’ belief that they need to do better when it comes to their offensive script. On Sunday, the club has relieved offensive coordinator Kellen Moore of his duties.
The #Cowboys and offensive coordinator Kellen Moore have mutually agreed to part ways, per sources.
