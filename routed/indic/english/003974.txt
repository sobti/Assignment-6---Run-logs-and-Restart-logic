Siddaramaiah meets kin of 22-year-old girl who died after being stuck in waterlogged underpass in Bengaluru A 22-year-old woman died after she and her five relatives got stuck inside a Multi-Purpose Vehicle (MPV) which was sunk in the flooded water at KR Circle underpass on Sunday evening. The rescue team from the fire and emergency services managed to save five of her relatives. Chief Minister Siddaramaiah said there were seven persons including the driver inside the vehicle. They were unable to come out as the doors were stuck. The victim Bhanu Rekha, a resident of Bengaluru and from Vijayawada in Andhra Pradesh, had drunk flood water and breathed her last before reaching a nearby private hospital.
