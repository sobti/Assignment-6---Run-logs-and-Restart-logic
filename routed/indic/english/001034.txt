DHUBRI: The concluding function of Dhubri Chhat Puja Committee was held with releasing of souvenir, prize distribution and cultural programmes at Dhubri District Library Auditorium on Sunday. The souvenir Sour Prabha, edited by Aditya Prakash Singh, was brought out by the committee and formally released jointly by the chairman of Assam Financial Corporation (AFC), Vijay Kumar Gupta and Dhubri Superintendent of Police who chaired the function as the special guest and chief guest respectively.
 Speaking at the function, Gupta spoke at a length in very lucid words on various aspects of social life and importance of social works in the society along with reciting his self-composed verses. “I am really happy to see the Dhubri Chhat Puja Committee is doing a lot of social work involving so many organizations, including ‘The Humanity’, an NGO known for blood donation. Besides these, the committee is also engaged in cultural and literary works which undoubtedly is commendable and laudable,” Gupta said.
 Terang, who in his brief speech praised the committee members for their unflinching dedication and selfless service during the celebration of Chhat Puja, said that was why the puja passed off peacefully without any untoward incident. The principal of St, Agastya High School, Narendra Kumar Sah praised the efforts by the committee in bringing out the souvenir and promised to contribute 25 percent of total cost of the souvenir from the next year. Members of all the organizations who extended their support and service, including individuals, in cleaning the ghats and other services were felicitated and prizes of art, quiz and extempore speech competitions held during Chhat Puja were also distributed among the winners followed by a cultural programme.
