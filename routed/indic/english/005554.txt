'Ungarala Rambabu', which hit the screens on Sept 15, has grossed Rs. 3. 60 Cr as of the end of Saturday, the makers have said in a statement.
 "If it has collected this much in just two days, then it means that our film is a sleeper hit like 'Jakkanna'. Our film is better of in terms of business compared to the other releases. The audiences are enjoying the wholesome package," the film's spokesperson says.
 The makers feel that Sunil's character and comedy, Prakash Raj's role, Vennela Kishore and others' comedy has worked in favour of the movie. The emotional content in terms of the hero's attitudes towards farmers has made a significant impact, the makers feel.
 Directed by Kranthi Madhav, the film also stars Miya George, Posani Krishna Murali and Ashish Vidyarthi.
