Saaho film took 2 years to complete and Prabhas after Baahubali-2 announced that he wants to do more films than doing one film in two years. But Saaho took more time than the team anticipated.
 It even cost them double the budget and Prabhas said that he does have a fear about the result of the film, recently.
 He said that he fears for his team that worked round the clock for 2 years and also his producers who spent huge amounts.
 He also fears for his audiences who want to get entertained and have huge expectations but he may fail them. So, he worked hard on the film for 2 years, even though that is lot of time and effort.
 He also said that Saaho could be his last big film in a while and he wants to do some simple medium budget films for 3-4 years and then attempt something big again.
 He said that this is his immediate film after a success like Baahubali-2 and he gave 7 years to three films, which he thinks worthy for the kind of films but he needs to have a change in pace, he feels.
 He wants to explore different genres and do variety regularly to not seem monotonous, it seems. He is busy promoting Saaho with Shraddha Kapoor all around the country.
