Pawan Kalyan interacted with Jana Sainiks who arrived from Vijayawada East and Narsapuram Constituencies at the party office in Mangalagiri. While addressing the cadre, He downplayed the criticism from VV Lakshminarayana who tendered his resignation citing his re-entry into Film Industry. 'It doesn't make any sense when Lakshminarayana says he quit Jana Sena just because I am signing films. I am doing this for the well-being of people dependent on Me and the party. I don't have assets or companies worth lakhs of crores. Whether those who are criticizing Me sacrificed at least 1,000 rupees for the sake of society? ,' he questioned.
 Jana Sena Chief targeted YS Jaganmohan Reddy by saying he became the Chief Minister begging the people for 'One Chance'. He didn't hesitate to say, 'Jagan has been ruining the state after getting hold of the power. YCP Leaders knew nothing other than Demolishing Structures, Abolition of Council, Shifting of Capital and Abusing Opposition Leaders'.
 PK accused YCP Government of denying Ration Cards and Housing Plots for the people who voted for Jana Sena and other Opposition Parties. He expressed concern over the drinking water issues faced by even people in the villages located on the banks of River Krishna.
 Jana Senani sees upcoming Local Body Polls as a good opportunity to strengthen the party at the grassroots level. He hopes strategies of Jana Sena will fetch good results for the party at the ward-level too.
