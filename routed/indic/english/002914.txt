Innovative types of interior design involve the use of the most modern finishing materials. They include, for example, 3d wall panels - an unusual and very effective look. Do you want to know more about him?
 3d panels are a cover that can be mounted on a wall, furniture, doors, columns , etc. They are great for zoning rooms, because due to their relief and unique color solution, these panels visually produce a three-dimensional effect. As a result, your room will look quite different than using traditional wall coverings.
 Wall panels in 3d format have the same advantages as conventional panels made of MDF, plastic or other materials. Let's enumerate what are the advantages in the design of the living area with such panels for the walls:
- aesthetics and modernity : any 3d cover now at the peak of popularity unlike walls with carpets or obsolete paper wallpapers;
- practical : the care of wall panels is easy. Separately, it should be noted their resistance to moisture, which makes it possible to use wall panels in any room. In addition, in the event of spoilage, you can easily replace one of the panels - it's much easier than pasting wallpaper or repainting the room;
- properties of heat and noise absorption , which reduce the cost of additional insulation work in this room;
- ecological compatibility and fire safety : most types of panels are made of high-quality materials, and the technologies for their production involve incombustibility and absolute safety;
- versatility : 3d wall panels suitable for kitchen, living room, nursery, bedroom, bathroom or any other room. You just need to choose the right design;
- exclusivity : decorating your apartment with three-dimensional panels, you are unlikely to see the same with your friends or neighbors. The choice of design for this material is very wide, and the specialists of the manufacturer always will be happy to help you choose exactly the kind of wall panels that will perfectly fit into the interior of your home and also it will be affordable for you.
 Of the minuses of the relief 3d wall panels we note only the cost, which largely depends on the material of their manufacture.
 Panels made of natural wood or veneer are expensive - and, in fact, they are. 3d wall panels made of wood are good for their natural beauty, which is not replaced by any artificial material.
 Much more democratic are the wall 3d MDF panels, which are durable and durable and also perfectly imitate different surfaces. They can be glossy or matte and have any coloring.
 Do not forget about such an interesting eco-material, like bamboo. If the style of your interior allows, you can use it as a primary accent or extra touches. 3d wall panels made of bamboo are very easy to mount, which makes it possible to install them yourself.
 Decorative aluminum is one of the most fashionable materials today. The panels made of it look exquisite and rich and at the same time easy. The relief of the construction is achieved through the processing of aluminum by engraving, anodizing and grinding.
 The plastic panels are less durable, but with careful care they will last you long enough. Plastic, covered with special PVC film, does not burn out in the sun, so that your 3d wall panels will look like new even after ten years.
 Glass panels 3d are often used to visually increase the space of a small room. Usually they are used in the kitchen interior.
 And, finally, gypsum wall panels 3d are no less popular. They have their own advantages, including absolute incombustibility, low weight and unique acoustic properties.
