Actress Taapsee Pannu, who has been in a diverse set of movies, right from serious ones such as Pink and Thappad to light-hearted ones likeJudwaa 2, has been very secretive of her personal life. In a recent interview with an online publication, Taapsee spoke of her families approval of her relationship.
She disclosed that her family's approval meant a lot to her, and it wouldn’t have worked had her family rejected her partner. According to reports, Taapsee is said to be dating badminton player from Denmark, Mathias Boe.
