Bollywood superstar Shah Rukh Khan, who is gearing up for the release of "Raees", will be seen promoting the action crime thriller film on popular reality show "Bigg Boss 10", which is hosted by Salman Khan. This is not the first time that King Khan will be promoting his film on 'Bigg Boss'. Shah Rukh and Salman recreated the "Karan Arjun" moment when they appeared together on an episode in the ninth season of "Bigg Boss", when Shah Rukh promoted "Dilwale". The promo of the episide, which will be a semi-finale episode of "Bigg Boss 10", is already out and was released by none other than Salman on his Twitter handle. In the episode, which will be aired on January 20, both the superstars will be seen having a lot of fun over interesting tasks and games.
