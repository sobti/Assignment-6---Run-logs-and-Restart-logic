Ford launched BS6-compliant Endeavour SUV in February this year at prices ranging between ₹29. 55 lakh and ₹33. 25 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi). The flagship Ford SUV featured no design changes as compared to its BS4 sibling but came with several mechanical changes. This includes a new engine and an automatic transmission option. Now, after over 5 months since the launch, Ford has decided to increase Endeavour's price in India. The entry-level Endeavour Titanium 4x2 AT trim now costs ₹29. 99 lakh, making it ₹44,000 costlier than before. Ford Endeavour Titanium+ 4X2 AT model is now costlier by ₹1. 2 lakh and gets a price tag of ₹32. 75 lakh while the top-spec Endeavour Titanium+ 4X4 AT will set you back by ₹34. 45 lakh instead of ₹33. 25 lakh. All prices are ex-showroom, Delhi.
 Ford Endeavour comes powered by a 2. 0-litre EcoBlue diesel engine that generates 168 bhp and a peak torque of 420 Nm. This powerplant is offered in combination with a ten-speed torque converter automatic transmission only, making it the only car in India to get a ten-speed automatic gearbox. Ford Endeavour 4X2 returns a claimed fuel efficiency of 13. 9 km/l while 4X4 variant claims fuel economy of 12. 4 km/l.
 Ford Endeavour comes loaded with a plethora of features like an eight-inch touchscreen infotainment system with FordPass suite, ambient lighting, handsfree tailgate opening system, push-button stop/start, 8-way powered front seats, dual-zone air conditioner, panoramic sunroof, semi-digital instrument panel, and terrain management system. The SUV also gets front, side, and curtain airbags, ABS with EBS, electronic stability control with rollover mitigation, cruise control, automatic headlamps, tyre pressure monitoring system, front parking sensors, etc.
