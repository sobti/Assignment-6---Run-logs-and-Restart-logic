The Supreme Court has reimposed stringent charges against an accused in the murder case of journalist Gauri Lankesh which were earlier quashed by the Karnataka High Court.
 By Nolan Pinto: The Supreme Court on Thursday set aside the Karnataka High Court order quashing stringent charges against an accused in the murder case of journalist Gauri Lankesh.
 Acting on a plea filed by Gauri Lankesh's sister Kavitha Lankesh challenging the high court verdict, a bench headed by Justice AM Khanwilkar restored the charges against accused Mohan Nayak under the Karnataka Control of Organised Crimes Act (KCOCA).
 "I am very happy. Some justice finally for all our fights. I am looking forward to read the order," Kavitha Lankesh said after the Supreme Court's order.
 In April this year, the Supreme Court had allowed the pleas filed by the state and Kavitha Lankesh. The Supreme Court reserved its judgment in the case in September.
 The Karnataka High Court had quashed the August 14, 2018 order of the police authority granting approval to invoke KCOCA for investigation against Mohan Nayak.
 Nayak was accused of harbouring Gauri Lankesh's killers and being a critical member of an "organised crime syndicate"
Gauri Lankesh was shot dead on the night of September 5, 2017 near her house in Rajarajeshwari Nagar in Bengaluru.
