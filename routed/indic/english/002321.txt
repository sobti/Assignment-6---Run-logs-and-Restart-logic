Former India spinner Harbhajan Singh praised the 23-year-old Rishabh Pant for his fearless batting display against England on Friday.
 By Rahul Bhatnagar: Former India spinner Harbhajan Singh called India wicketkeeper-batsman Rishabh Pant a special player and applauded him for his third Test hundred.
 “Special player special 100 @RishabhPant17 #INDvENG #Gamechanger keep going champion,” Harbhajan Singh said on Twitter.
 Rishabh Pant on Friday scored his third Test hundred in the ongoing match against England to bail his team out of trouble in at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad.
 India were struggling on 80/4 when Pant came in to bat, still far behind England’s first innings score of 205. He eventually departed on 101, pulling England fast bowler James Anderson to Joe Root at short midwicket. India was at 259/6 when he departed, 54 runs ahead of the visitors.
 Pant brought up his century with a six off England spinner Joe Root and was applauded by former India batsman Sunil Gavaskar for the way he got to his century. Gavaskar went on to say that this is what bold India is.
 Pant on Friday was batting on 94 when he went down on one knee to Joe Root’s spin and hit it over square leg for a six.
 “It has come at such a crucial time for India. You asked me a question what you think he is going to do and I said that he will get to his hundred with a six, and that’s exactly what he has done. This is the bold India, this is India that believes in itself.
 “This is the India that we all love, we all love. Look at that, look at the way he has given joy to those at the ground, to the millions watching this on television. Oh, this is amazing. This is so wonderful,” said Sunil Gavaskar while doing commentary on Star Sports.
