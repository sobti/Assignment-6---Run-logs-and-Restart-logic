Hyderabad FC secures three more points in their Durand Cup 2022 campaign.
Bartholomew Ogbeche stars as Hyderabad FC come from behind to win 3-1 against Chennaiyin FC. It was Anirudh Thapa's first half goal which gave Chennai the lead, but not for long.
At around the 56th minute, Joao Victor slotted a penalty home after defender Fallou Diagne was seen handling the ball inside the box, which compelled the referee to show him a red card.
After Ogbeche came on, he promptly gave the lead to his team. After this, Chennaiyin had lost all their vigour which was present in the first half. After the Nigerian scored his second, the final nail in Chennai's coffin was hammered in.
