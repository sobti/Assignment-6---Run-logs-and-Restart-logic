A B. Sc second-year graduate who merged victoriously as a first-time ward member made many heads turn after the ruling Left Democratic Front(LDF) has picked her as the Thiruvananthapuram Municipal Corporation.
 21-year-old Arya Rajendran is the Mayor-elect of the Thiruvananthapuram Municipal Corporation. She has sworn in as the Mayor today.
 Celebrities and noted faces across the fields like politics, entertainment, and business have extended their wishes to her and wished the young lad will inspire others as well.
 Congressman and Thiruvananthapuram MP Shashi Tharoor, who is known for his extraordinary English vocabulary has heaped praise on Arya Rajendran, youngest Mayor of India.
 The chairman and founder of the Adani group, Gautam Adani shared a wonderful note for Arya Rajendran. In his tweet, he termed the victory, stunning and said this is the way young political leaders inspire others.
 Malayalam Super Star Mohan Lal has also wished Arya Rajendran. The actor called the Mayor-elect and wished her in their phone conversation. The Stalwart is also a voyager from Mudavanmugal ward, where Arya stood in elections and emerged victoriously.
