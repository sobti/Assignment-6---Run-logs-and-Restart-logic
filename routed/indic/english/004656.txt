The crimes committed against First Nations in residential schools and Indian Day Schools that ended in 1996 need to be addressed. It is time for the people responsible to have somekind of accountability and legal action brought forward. Legal investigations need to be carried out ASAP and charges laid. Our First Nations/Indigenous communities have been subjected to the reality of rape, starvation, abuse both physical and mental and murder, no one has been held accountable. Its time, please join us with this petition to have our voices heard by our government and help support putting those responsible behind bars.
