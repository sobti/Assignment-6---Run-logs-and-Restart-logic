Kalki Koechlin wishes to work with 'Khans'
'Happy Ending' actress Kalki Koechlin shows interest to work with Khans of the industry. Recently at an interview, this stunning star opened up saying that she would love to work with the Khans, especially with King Khan - Shah Rukh Khan because she finds him fantastic and charming.
 Talented Kalki also says that she would love to try her hands on love stories as it's not about not wanting to do commercial roles, but something that she can pull off happily. She also thinks that Vishal Bhardwaj is one of the outstanding filmmakers as she finds him to be both good in artistic alternatives and commercial cinema.
 Kalki is currently working with Nicholas Kharkongor's directorial 'Mantra' that features Shiv Pandit and Rajat Kapoor in the lead.
