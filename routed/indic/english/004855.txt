 Digital Desk: This information is for you if you are a fan of the popular web series Breaking Bad. Do you recall the white underpants that Walter White, played by Bryan Cranston, wore in the pilot episode while making crystal meth? Fans now have the opportunity to purchase the recognisable undergarment. You did read that correctly.
 So, along with other pieces from the show's memorabilia, Propstore Auction has offered the pair of underpants on their website as part of the Los Angeles Online Entertainment Memorabilia Auction. Over a thousand TV and entertainment props will be auctioned off, according to Sky News.
 The white underwear has received 14 bids so far and is predicted to bring in USD 5000, or roughly Rs 4,13,310.
 On February 13, the ad on the Propstore Auction website went up, and it will remain up until February 27.

 Throughout the series, coordinating underwear also showed up in his closet "listed stated.
