PESHAWAR: Law Enforcement Agencies (LEA) conducted search operations in various part of Peshawar under National Action Plan (NAP) and arrested over 40 suspects here on Tuesday.
According to police, LEA conducted search operations in different parts of the city including Hayatabad and nabbed over 40 suspects including Afghan residents who were living illegally.
Police have claimed that arrested suspected have not documents of rent.
LEA recovered weapon from their possession and moved them to Hayatabad jail for further inquiry.
