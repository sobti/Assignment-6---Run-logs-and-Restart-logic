Skoda Rapid TSI AT vs Skoda Rapid TSI MT: The automatic variants are pricier than their manual counterparts in the range of Rs 1. 30 lakh to Rs 1. 50 lakh.
 - The Skoda Rapid TSI AT is priced between Rs 9. 49 lakh and Rs 13. 29 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi).
 - The Skoda Rapid TSI MT price ranges from Rs 7. 49 lakh to Rs 11. 79 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi).
 - The Rapid TSI AT is available in five variants, while the Rapid TSI MT gets six.
 By Varun Singh: Skoda Auto India today launched the Rapid TSI AT in the country. Introduced in five variants, the Skoda Rapid TSI AT price starts at Rs 9. 49 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi) and goes up to Rs 13. 29 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi). Apart from the Skoda Rapid TSI AT, the petrol automatic options in the mid-size sedan segment include the Honda City CVT, the Hyundai Verna DCT and IVT, the Maruti Suzuki Ciaz AT, the Volkswagen Vento AT and the Toyota Yaris CVT-i.
 The Skoda Rapid TSI is powered by a BS6-compliant, 3-cylinder, 1. 0-litre, TSI (turbocharged stratified injection) petrol engine that is good for 110PS of maximum power and 175Nm of peak torque. While a 6-speed manual gearbox was available from the moment the new Rapid TSI entered India in May 2020, it now gets an automatic transmission option in the form of a 6-speed torque converter. Skoda Auto India claims a mileage of 18. 97kmpl for the Rapid TSI MT and 16. 24kmpl for the Rapid TSI AT.
 Unlike the Skoda Rapid TSI MT, which is available in six variants (Rider, Rider Plus, Ambition, Onyx, Style and Monte Carlo), the Skoda Rapid TSI AT has five variants (Rider Plus, Ambition, Onyx, Style and Monte Carlo).
