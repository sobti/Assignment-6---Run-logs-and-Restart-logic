Several WWE Superstars have returned since Triple H replaced Vince McMahon as the company's creative figurehead in July 2022. Gene Snitsky, a main roster talent between 2004 and 2008, wants to face Braun Strowman or Roman Reigns if he ever makes a WWE comeback.
 Snitsky, 53, is best remembered for feuding with Kane in 2004. The towering superstar has been likened to Strowman due to his facial hair and imposing presence. His appearance has also received comparisons to Bray Wyatt.
