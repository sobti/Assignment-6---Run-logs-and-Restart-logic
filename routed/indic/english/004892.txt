Anushka Shetty starrer Nishabdham is currently in post production stage. Though the shoot of Nishabdham has been wrapped long ago, but currently the team is busy in the post production work. Kona Venkat and Gopi Mohan penned the script while a debutant Hemanth Madhukar helmed this multilingual drama Nishabdham. Initially Nishabdham was slated to hit the theaters in the month of January but later the makers postponed it’s release. It was heard that Nishabdham might release on the 20th of February. But now if ongoing buzz in media and film industry is to be believed, Nishabdham has been postponed to April, and the makers are eyeing the 2nd of April to release the film. Finally the makers chose Summer slot for Nishabdham.
The sources say that the delay in the extensive VFX works for the film was the major reason for the postponement. The team of Bishabdham needs more time in order to get the VFX right. Even though the first copy of Anushka Shetty starrer will be readied by 28th February, the makers have shifted the release of the film keeping in mind the exam season. As Nishabdham caters to all age groups, the team has decided to release it on 2nd April.
Apart from Anushka Shetty, the film Nishabdham also has Michael Madsen, Madhavan, Shalini Pandey, and Anjali, in the supporting roles. The movie Nishabdham has been shot predominantly in Seattle, and is bankrolled by Kona Venkat and TG Vishwa Prasad.
