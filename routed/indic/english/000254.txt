Adivi Sesh is now preparing for the release of Telugu Goodachari 2, the follow-up to his spy action thriller Goodachari. The movie came out after Major and HIT: The Second Case. Sobhita Dhulipala, Jagapathi Babu, and Prakash Raj were also featured in the movie.
Adivi will be present at a significant event that Goodachari 2 or G2's makers have scheduled in Mumbai to promote the movie on a massive scale.
Abhishek Agarwal, the director of The Kashmir Films, Major, and Karthikeya 2, will soon be seen making Goodachari 2. A young intelligence officer who seeks to get revenge for the death of his father was portrayed in the franchise's first movie, which came out in 2018. However, he is also accused of killing his superiors at the same time. Consequently, his task becomes even more difficult.
Adivi Sesh was last spotted in HIT 2 on the big screen. He appeared in the Homicide Intervention Team as police officer Krishna Dev. Meenakshi Chaudhary plays the lead role in the film.
