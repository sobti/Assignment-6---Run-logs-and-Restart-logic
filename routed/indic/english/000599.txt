I have booked the Zeta model. Yes, it is a good car for Family. Surely not for short city rides where you have to pray or fight for the parking lot. The only drawback which I noticed in the car is the middle row captain seats. 1. The seats are flat like a bench which cannot hold the body on brakings. we tend to go forward in the sitting position every time of more than medium braking. The space around the seats are amazing but this should be taken care of 2. The arm resting position on the door is truly awkward where I was not able to rest it for more than 5 minutes as there are hard plastics which are not so well designed to rest your hands comfortably without causing pain.
