Gautham Vasudev Menon's latest film 'Vendhu Thanindhadhu Kaadu' starring Simbu and produced by Ishari Ganesh received critical acclaim and is also said to be a huge commercial hit. The film written by Jeyamohan has music by A. R. Rahman is entering its third week in theaters worldwide.
 Producer Ishari Ganesh apparently elated by the tremendous success of 'Vendhu Thanindhadhu Kaadu' held a success meet party for the cast and crew members. Sivakarthikeyan was the special invitee. The Vels Group head gifted a new Toyota Vellfire luxury car to Simbu and the latest Bullet bike to GVM. A video of Ishari Ganesh riding pillion to GVM on the new motorbike has gone viral on the internet.
 It has already been announced that Simbu, GVM, ARR, Jeyamohan and Ishari Ganesh will once again collaborate for 'Vendhu Thanindhadhu Kaadu 2'. The scripting process in on and will depict the rise of Simbu's character Muthu as the don of dons in Mumbai.
