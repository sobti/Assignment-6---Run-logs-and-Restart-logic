YSRCP gave so many tickets to the higher educated people in these elections.
 All these candidates entered the electoral field after excelling in their respective fields. The party gave tickets to these kind of people at several constituencies in the state.
 These persons include IAS,IPS, doctors, advocates, professors and others. Some of them came from the corporate sector too leaving behind their huge salaries.
 Kottapalli Gita, who worked as a Deputy Collector, is now the party candidate from Araku parliamentary constituency.
 Reddy Santhi, is now a candidate from Srikakulam parliamentary constituency. Her husband is an IFS officer.
 Golla Babu Rao, who is contesting from Amalapuram parliamentary constituency, worked as CEO of Zilla Parishad.
