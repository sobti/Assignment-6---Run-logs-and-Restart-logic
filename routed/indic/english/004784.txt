A giant and mysterious blob has been discovered by National park officials on a North Carolina beach.
 The photos of the object have gone viral and shocked netizens but experts suggest the 'mysterious mass' could probably be egg sacs from a squid.
 The strange mass washed up on the shore a few months ago but its photos were only released online recently after experts appealed for the public's help in trying to identify it.
 The Cape Lookout National Seashore posted a photo of it on Facebook, asking its followers to identify the gelatinous mass.
 “Beach mystery -- Do you know what this mysterious mass is? It was found a few months ago on the beach. So far it has escaped being identified - although it might be something like the egg sacks of a squid (but we aren't sure). Anyone want to take a stab at identifying it for us? ” Cape Lookout National Seashore wrote on Facebook.
 The post has over 70 shares and netizens have responded to it by commenting what they think the strange object might be.
 A user wrote, "Common European squid egg sacs (Alloteuthis / Loligo subulata), the Closest thing I found"
"Egg mass of an inshore squid, family Loliginidae. Same family as the Calif market squid. Three species are common in NC. These look like Lolliguncula brevis but could be either Doryteuthis pealeii or D. plei," wrote another.
 "Squid eggs. The sacs can be found in a cluster, akin to a bunch of grapes, or as individuals. If observed closely, you can just make out a honeycomb pattern within," wrote a third user.
 It's pretty clear that most users believe the blob is an egg sac of a squid or some other creature.
 Some users suggested that the photo be posted on Reddit for more convincing answers and explanations.
