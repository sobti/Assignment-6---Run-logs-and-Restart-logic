The premiere of Stone Cold Steve Austin's 'The Broken Skull Sessions' is almost upon us, as the show will stream on the WWE Network right after Survivor Series. The WWE Hall of Famer recently talked with Corey Graves on After The Bell, and revealed why he was nervous while interviewing The Undertaker for the first episode.
 The Undertaker has had one of the most iconic careers in all of Pro Wrestling, and an argument could be made for The Deadman being the greatest character in the history of this business. Years after The Undertaker's debut, Steve Austin rose up the ranks in WWE and was a major reason why WWE managed to defeat WCW in the Monday Night Wars. On the other hand, The Undertaker was a mainstay in WWE for almost three decades, and is a guaranteed first-ballot future Hall of Famer.
 While talking with Graves, Austin opened up on why he was nervous while interviewing Mark Calaway, the man behind The Undertaker.
 Well, yeah, a little bit, I'm not going to lie to you, it's the first one out of the gate. I'm used to my one man podcast, shootin' the breeze, all of sudden you throw in all the production values that WWE throws in and it looks like a million bucks. It takes it to a whole another level, so you want to do the best you can, but also you're taken aback just a little bit. I think we worked through that and I'm proud of it.
