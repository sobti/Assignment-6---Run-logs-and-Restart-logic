Actress Rachitha is turning out to be one of the hot favorites of the ongoing 'Bigg Boss Tamil 6' on Vijay TV. On one side Robert trying romantic overtures on her have provided content for the show while on the other hand her fair game is winning her more fans.
The nomination process for this week's eviction was held and all the contestants entered the conference room and nominated two of their housemates. Rachita also named her two contestants and waited for Bigg Boss's response. Sasho who voices 'Bigg Boss' said 'You can go'. But Rachitha still kept waiting and said that she wanted him to say in his regular style "You can go Rachitha". There was a long silence and then Bigg Boss obliged her by saying it as she wanted and the pretty actress went out with a smile.
This fun video has gone viral and Rachitha's fans are calling it super cute and are lauding her for trolling 'Bigg Boss' himself. Already the Rachitha army on social media is confident that she can win the title easily. Lets wait and see how that plays out in the coming weeks.
