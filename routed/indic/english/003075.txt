A woman was arrested for attacking her live-in partner's family with a knife after her lover went missing two days ago in Madhya Pradesh's Gwalior.
 By Ravish Pal Singh: A woman from Madhya Pradesh's Gwalior was arrested for a knife attack on her live-in partner's family.
 It all started when Ram Lakhan Batham went missing. The woman who was in a live-in relationship with Ram Batham felt his family had sent him away.
 The woman then reached his house and stabbed his mother in the neck with a knife. Batham's father, who tried to prevent the attack, also sustained injuries.
 According to the police, Ram Batham went missing two days ago.
 However, the woman accused of the knife attack is already married but has been living with Ram Batham.
 A case has been registered, and a search is on to find the man who remains missing.
