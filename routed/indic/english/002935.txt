Maktab Jamiya Salamaniya Khatoon Janath at Lashkar Mohalla has organised Mehfil Zikro Azkar Majlees celebration and lecture programme on Fazilath-e-Shabe Barath in connection with the 14th Shaban-e-Mouzam Hijri 1442.
 The event will be held today (Mar. 25) at 8. 30 pm after Isha Salath under the supervision of Hazarath Moulana Sufi Mohammed Anwar Ahmed Nizami, Secretary of Darul Uloom Azeezia Education Trust, Mysuru. Devotees are urged to wear face masks, maintain social distancing and use hand sanitiser. For details, contact Ph: 0821-25202787, according to a press release.
