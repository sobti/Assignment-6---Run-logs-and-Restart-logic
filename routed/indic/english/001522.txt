After 'Saptagiri Express' and 'Saptagiri LLB', Saptagiri is now going to try his luck with an action-comedy titled 'Gajadonga'.
 "The story has nothing to do with senior NTR garu's film of the same title," producers Sharma Chukka, Edala Narendra and GVK Reddy say. The film's director, D Ramakrishna, has previously worked for Geetha Arts and Super Good Films.
 "We are going to make a one-of-its-kind Action entertainer that tells the story of a thief who nabs real thieves in our society," the director says.
 'Gajadonga' will have the backdrop of a village and a semi-urban area. To be shot from August, it will have music by Bulganin and cinematography by Praveen Vanamali. Art direction is by Varma. Scripted by GTR Mahendra and PV Sathish, it has story and screenplay by the director himself.
