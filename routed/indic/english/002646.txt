After all big films have hit the floors, Icon star Allu Arjun fans have been eagerly waiting for the update Pushpa shooting details. Earlier, it was speculated to resume in the second week of July. But it hasn’t been resumed.
 According to the film nagar sources, the director Sukumar was infected by viral fever and the shooting of Pushpa will be resumed only after the Rangasthalam director has fully recovered from the fever.
 The pending portion of Pushpa first part will be started in August and expecting some exciting updates from the team. FaFa(Fahadh Faasil) will be seen in a key role in this pan India film. It’s a Rockstar DSP musical and it’s a Mythri Movie Makers venture.
