As of July 2023, American actor James Franco has a net worth of $30 million as per the specialized website Celebrity Net Worth.
James Franco is an American actor and filmmaker who is best known for playing the role of Harry Osborne in Sam Raimi’s Spider-Man. Franco has done a great number of films over the years, the best ones are the films that he did with actor Seth Rogen - films such as Sausage Party, Pineapple Express, This Is the End, and The Disaster Artist. Franco has won a Golden Globe Award for Best Actor for his role in 127 Hours.
It has been a while since Franco has been on screen, this comes after the controversy in which he agreed that he has sex with students who attend his film classes. He has been hit by lawsuits and has done a $2 million sexual-misconduct settlement in 2021.
As mentioned earlier, James Franco's net worth is noted to be $30 million. Franco was born in Palo Alto, California on April 19, 1978, to Douglas Eugene Franco and Betsy Lou. He has two other brothers, Dave and Tom Franco, who are actors themselves. Franco has been interested in acting since his high school days.
He used to act in plays and that's when he decided to follow the acting path. He then attended the CSSA in 1998 for theater studies. He began his career by starring as a guest star in Pacific Blue in 1997. Franco's then breakthrough role was in the comedy-drama TV series, Freaks and Geeks.
Franco has worked in numerous films such as Spider-Man, The Company, The Ape, James Dean, Tristan & Isolde, Flyboys, 127 Hours, Rise of the Planes of the Apes, Your Highness, and many more.
