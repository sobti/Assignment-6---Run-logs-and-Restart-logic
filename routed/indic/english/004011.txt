Maharashtra government will fill up 40 thousand vacant posts in all Municipal Corporations, Municipal Councils, and Nagar Panchayats of the state. Chief Minister Eknath Shinde has directed to immediately fill up these vacant posts in all civic bodies.
 Addressing a conference of top officials of civic bodies, Mr. Shinde said, more than 55 thousand posts are vacant in all municipal corporations and councils in the state.
