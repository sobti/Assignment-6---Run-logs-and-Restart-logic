Christmas video goes viral; Emirates A380 transformed into Santa's sleigh drawn by reindeer.
 Emirates has turned one of its A380 aircraft into Santa's sleigh in an effort to spread holiday cheer.
 The Dubai airline collaborated with digital artist Mostafa Eldiasty, also known on Instagram as 100. Pixels, to produce a spectacular film of an aeroplane being towed by reindeer over the runway at Dubai International Airport, just in time to celebrate Christmas. .
 The video, which has gone popular on social media, shows the reindeer taking off and then soaring above the cityscape of Dubai while the jet is topped with a Santa hat.
 The video is widely shared. It has generated a lot of holiday cheer in the comments, where people have called it fantastic and quite imaginative.
