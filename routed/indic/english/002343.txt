Tiger Shroff, Ananya Pandey and Tara Sutaria starrer Student of the Year 2 has been released in cinemas. The film is a sequel to the 2012 hit film ‘Student Of The Year’ which marked the Bollywood debut of Alia Bhatt, Varun Dhawan, and Sidharth Malhotra. Here is Student of the Year 2 review which might help you to watch it.
Story of the movie:
Rohan (Tiger) a student, athlete and kabaddi player from the quotidian Pishorilal Chamandas College, who is infatuated with his childhood sweetheart, Mia(Tara) aspires to become the ‘Student Of The Year’ by winning the Inter-College Dignity Cup. He also follows Mia to the affluent St Teresa College courtesy his sports scholarship. However, he is belittled by Manav (Aditya), the college-trustee’s son, who is the reigning college champ. Manav’s sister, the utterly-spoilt Shreya (Ananya) falls for Rohan. Things take an ugly turn when their life gets tangled by failure, manipulation and heartbreak. Rohan is rusticated from St Teresa and goes back to Pishorilal College. Bruised and battered, Rohan, the underdog challenges Manav to clinch the coveted SOTY trophy.
Star Performance:
Tiger has a charismatic screen presence, but he hardly looks like a college kid. Ananya Pandey looks perfect in her role. While Tara Sutaria was okay in her role. Aditya Seal is decent but has nothing much to contribute to the film.

Music Direction:
The music album is one of the weakest by Vishal-Shekhar. There isn’t a single song you could take away from the film.
The film’s direction is good. The script of the film could be even better. There isn’t a single moment in the film that could be described as passable.
