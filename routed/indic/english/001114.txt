Sakura used Naruto, repeatedly Naruto was crazy about Sakura initially until he found his true love in Hinata who always loved him. However, Sakura just used Naruto for her selfish gain. She wanted him everytime she was in trouble.
 Too much of Hashirama cells The concept of the Hashirama cell was cool and all, but that overshadowed those who became powerful without it. Danzo, Madara, and others used the cells to covet power, but didn't it lessen their individual strength?
 What could possibly kill Hashirama Senju Where can we file a petition to learn of Hashirama's death? Given how powerful he was, his endurance and healing power, what could be the possible reason that claimed the life of such a powerful being?
 Purpose of fillers was lost Anime fans are already split in their views over fillers, and Naruto has too many. These light episodes are there so writers could take a while for story progression, but it did not appear with Naruto.
 Sasuke was not punished for what he did, why? Sasuke became a criminal with true intensions of ending human life unlike Itachi who took down his whole to save Konoha. But he was never punished for that. May we ask why?
 Talk-no-jutsu was good, but. . . Talk-no-jutsu, as fans call it, is the strongest trick of Naruto, which puts every war at an end. But every war? Really? Even in the great ninja war, he convinced Obito and others to choose a different path, but that seems unreal when you are in the middle of a war.
