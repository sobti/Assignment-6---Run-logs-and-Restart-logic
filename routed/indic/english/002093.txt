Even though India and China came up with peaceful thoughts, Chinese Government is trying its best to find a loophole to start the attack. Most of the times, China is trying to provoke India into a tension situation. In fact, China feels that India should frighten for its power but India is not ready to step back. More over, India is completely prepared for any attack across the border.
 China now came up with a plan of using air attack first in case of war. Few fifth generation J20 fighters were stationed 150kms away from India border. These are stealth fighter jets that are quite immune to the radar system. They were said to be the most superior fighter jets on China’s side.
 On the other hand, India has deployed Su-30, MIG-29k, drones and surveillance flights on the border. Between India and China, around 1 lakh soldiers were deployed in the border.
