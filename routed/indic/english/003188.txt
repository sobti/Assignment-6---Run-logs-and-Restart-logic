With the temperature across Delhi crossing the 45-degrees Celsius mark and power supply playing truant, citizens have been having a harrowing time over the past few days. To top it all, there appears to be no respite in sight just yet from the scorching heat.
 Since Sunday evening, electricity has failed multiple times in several areas.
 “There was no electricity for almost three hours. My dad is unwell and his health gets affected due to this,” said Madan Lal, a resident of Brahmpuri in East Delhi.
 Shilpi, a resident of C block, Ram Dutt Enclave, Uttam Nagar, had a similar grievance. “We have been battling load shedding for the last three to four days. While in the last few days, there were power cuts for three to four hours, last night there was a blackout in our colony from 11 in the night till 7 a. m. Even inverters fail in such long cuts,” she said.
 The complaints regarding power outages came from across the city. “After a long day of work, I was looking forward to have a rest when the power went off,” said Shourish Sarkar, a media professional who resides in Bhogal.
