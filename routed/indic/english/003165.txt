Institute of Management Technology (IMT), Hyderabad is organizing annual management Fest- IMPELZ on December 5 and 6. Students from popular business schools like IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Raipur, IIM Kozhikode, ISB, XIMB, SIBM Pune, Welingkar and other B Schools will participate in the events.
 Owing to its grand success in 2014, IMT Hyderabad has planned to organize IMPELZ “Bigger and Better” this year. There are around 11 clubs, each for a different domain like Finance, HR, Marketing, Operations, Strategy, Cultural, Social, Sports, Entrepreneurship and Communications.
 At IMPELZ, all the clubs of the institute collaborate to host events where the participants will be given opportunities to test their management skills against the smartest brains from all over the country. The winners will be given cash prizes, certificates and accolades.
 The preliminary rounds for the events about 260 teams participated from various B-Schools. IMPELZ 2015 will start off with an inaugural address by Mr. Suresh Rayudu Chitturi, Vice Chairman and Director of Srinivasa Hatcheries Group.
 All the clubs of the institute will be organizing one major event pertaining to real time business scenario, providing a platform for the participants to exhibit their management skills.
