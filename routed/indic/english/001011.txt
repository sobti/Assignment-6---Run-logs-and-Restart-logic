The moment you’ve all been waiting for is finally here! It’s time to announce the winners of War of Words, the competition organized by Sportskeeda as part of Sprint’13 – the annual sports fest of SP Jain Institute of Management and Research.
 The winner of the 1st prize is vikramshah for his article “Sachin’s Children – Memories of Master and India’s Second Coming“.
 The 2nd prize goes to Prateek Jose for his article “IOA Suspension: A blessing undisguised“.
 And lastly, the 3rd prize goes to Varun Chand for his article “Through the Looking Glass Part-II: Club Ownership and Beyond“.
 Heartiest congratulations to the three winners – your well-deserved prizes will reach you very shortly!
