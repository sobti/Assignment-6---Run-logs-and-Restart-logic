The government has imposed the lockdown in seven districts to contain current spread of Coronavirus.
 The lockdown will remain active from June 22 to June 30.
 The districts are Manikganj, Munshiganj, Narayanganj, Gazipur, Rajbari, Madaripur and Gopalganj.
 Cabinet Secretary Khandaker Anwarul Islam said this at an emergency press briefing at Secretariat on Monday afternoon.
