Ganjam: In a shocking incident of crude bomb blast, a miscreant got killed while his accomplice sustained critical injuries in the district here today.
The deceased has been identified as Mantu Maharana alias Kalia and the critically injured youth has been identified as Silu Pahan.
Sources said that they were on their way on a motorbike to commit crime somewhere when the crude bomb they possessed slipped off hand killing Kalia on the spot near Nirmaljhar under Khalikot Police Station (PS) limits.
The intensity of the bomb blast was so strong that the right hand of the deceased got split and was found lying far away from the body beside the highway.
Being informed, the local police rushed there and initiated investigation. Injured Silu was also admitted to the hospital.
