Salman Khan never came out in the pen to speak about any issue, unless it hurt him or threatened to jeopardise his career. To even do that, he uses other methods to convey his thoughts. For instance the Big Boss in which he is participating or the Kapil Sharma Show which he produces, he uses them to make a statement and expects people to read between the lines. Meanwhile his PR machinery goes on an overdrive trying to make the point clear even before the show ends. This time he came down heavily on Arnab Goswami in both the programmes albeit indirectly and even said that he had spoken something indirectly that has to be a take-away. Salman Khan during Bigg Boss 14, over the weekend, took a sly dig at some media channels who are allegedly manipulating TRPs.
He further added, “Jo mujhe kehna tha, indirectly maine keh diya (What I wanted to say, I have said indirectly).” For the uninformed, a fake TRP case was registered by Mumbai Police last week. It has claimed to have busted a TRP manipulation racket after TV viewership measurement agency Broadcast Audience Research Council (BARC) filed a complaint.
