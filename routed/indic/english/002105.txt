 Kerala Public Service Commission, KPSC has released Kerala PSC Provisional Answer Key 2021 of common preliminary examinations. The examination was conducted offline across various exam centres in the state on November 13, 2021. Candidates who have appeared in the prelims exam can now check answer keys and apply for objections, if any, on KPSC Thulasi login at keralapsc. gov. in.
 The exam was earlier scheduled to be held on October 23 and 30, 2021. However, it got postponed later due to some unavoidable administrative reasons. Candidates must raise objections, if any, within a period of 5 days from the date of publication of Kerala PSC Provisional Answer Key 2021.
 The results and final answer keys would be based entirely on the objections raised by the candidates. The steps and direct link to download Kerala PSC Answer Key 2021 is shared below.
 - Visit the official website of the Kerala Public Service Commission, KPSC, at keralapsc. gov. in.
 - Go to the 'Downloads' menu and scroll down to select 'Answer Key - OMR exams'.
 - On a new redirected window, select an appropriate link or click on direct links - Common Preliminary Examination (Degree Level) - Stage I (TAMIL) or (KANNADA).
 - In case of any objections, candidates must visit KPSC Thulasi Login and apply through the portal.
 The short notice on the official website reads, "The Final Answer Key, modified as per experts' opinion on the valid complaints received from candidates within the stipulated time, will be published in the Bulletin and website. The complaints received after the publication of Final Answer Key will not be entertained. " In case of any queries, candidates can reach out to the Help Desk at 0471-2546400 or kpsc. psc@kerala. gov. in.
