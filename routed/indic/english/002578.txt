Webb Simpson is excited to have rejoined the PGA Tour after a coronavirus scare in his family. Simpson: "So we felt great but obviously a lot of support. Just from everyone wishing my daughter well, and it's a bit odd I feel like this pandemic, sometimes people have it and they don't have any symptoms, or a case like my daughter's who we don't believe had it and you're getting these well-wishes. So it's a bit of an odd time but happy to be here at the Rocket Mortgage. I love this golf course, grew up a great Donald Ross fan, he's a great designer. So it feels great to be here, great to be playing again and look forward to starting tomorrow."
