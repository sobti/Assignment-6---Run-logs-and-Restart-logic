Please be informative and creative while creating post for blog. Sometimes I have seen some students are posting only few lines in their posts or posting few links to other sites. Please note that we can not keep those posts as it will not be good for our blog site's quality. If you want to tell us about a site please write what you have found in the site, why do you think the site is good and how do you think the site will help us in a paragraph. Then you can direct us the link.
Also note that blog is not a mare conversation site. First you have to post a quality post and people will post their feedback and comments on it. you can discuss about the post through the comments afterward.
I hope this helps to remove some of the confusions about blog posting.
