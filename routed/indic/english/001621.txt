The global coronavirus pandemic disrupted the functioning of the movie industry. During this pandemic, some films skipped the theatrical release for a direct digital release. Here is a Tamil movie which is having a direct Television premiere instead of a theatrical or OTT release. Naanga Romba Busy is the title of the film, produced by popular filmmaker Sundar C.
Naanga Romba Busy will not have a theatrical release and the TV premiere will take place on SUN TV on the occasion of Diwali. Sun TV has secured worldwide digital and satellite rights of the movie. After premiering the film on TV, the movie will be released on the SUN Nxt platform. The final leg of the film’s shoot is in progress and the post-production works are taking place simultaneously.Naanga Romba Busy is the official remake of the Kannada movie Mayabazar.
Prasanna, Shyam, Shruthi Marathe, Ashwin, Yogibabu, VTV Ganesh, RNR. Manohar, Rithika Sen, and Master Shakti acted in the lead roles of the movie. Sundar C produced the movie under the Avni Movies banner. Badri who earlier directed Veerappu is the director of Naan Romba Busy.
