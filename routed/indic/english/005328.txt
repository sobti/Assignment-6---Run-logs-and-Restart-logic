Mukul Kumar, Muzaffarpur: In a disturbing case from Muzaffarpur, Bihar, a man kidnapped a minor girl from a village in the Kanti police station area with the goal of marrying her. Local police detained the accused, Nitish Kumar Ram, in the crime. The arrested suspect is a Virpur resident. A police report was filed against him at the local police station.
 The girl’s family had filed a complaint with the local Kanti police station. The relatives claimed in their plea that Nitish Kumar abducted a juvenile girl with the intent of marrying her. After receiving the report from the relatives, the police conducted constant raids to apprehend the perpetrator.
 The authorities received a tip that both the accused and the abducted girl were in the same location, attempting to flee the state. The police detained both of them quickly.
 The local police have found the abducted girl, and after recording the girl’s statement under CrPC Section 164, the police are preparing to take further legal action.
