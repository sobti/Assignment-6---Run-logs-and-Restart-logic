Pawan Kalyan kept applying breaks to his Porata Yatra in Uttar Andhra once in few days. Earlier, He confined himself to closed door discussions for two days because of security issues and even took up a day-long hunger strike for Uddanam Kidney Victims. And now, PK decided to stop the Yatra for one week. What could be the reason?
 Majority of the personal security of Pawan Kalyan are Muslims. So, Pawan took the decision to stop the yatra for now and resume it after the Ramzan festival.
 After the public meeting at Yalamanchili, Pawan Kalyan reached Sai Priya Resorts at Bheemi Beach Road. He had meetings with Intellectuals, Experts and Teachers on Saturday. During the break period, PK would be focusing on showing about the backwardness of Uttar Andhra by interacting with people from different walks of life. He would catch the Hyderabad-bound Flight only on Monday evening.
 Sources say, Frequent breaks for Porata Yatra is a strategic move. Pawan Kalyan has been trying to use this period for choosing the right candidates for 2019 Polls. He seems to be keen to know about the strengths & weakness of the opponents before fielding Jana Sena Candidates.
