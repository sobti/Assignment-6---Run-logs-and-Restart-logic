Police say they were called to the Dockside Drive and Queen’s Quay East area just before 4 p.m. for a reported stabbing.
Upon arrival, officers located a victim with stab wounds. They were transported to hospital with serious but non-life-threatening injuries, according to police. Police say the stabbing took place in the vicinity of George Brown’s Waterfront Campus, but could not confirm if it happened on college property.
