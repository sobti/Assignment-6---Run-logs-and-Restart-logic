New rumours suggest another version of the Galaxy Grand is on the way, named Samsung Galaxy Grand Neo. The Galaxy Grand Neo is believed to be just below the Galaxy Grand 2 in the company’s smartphone line up.
The smartphone is believed to have a 1.2GHz quad-core A7 CPU along with a 5-inch WVGA display. It remains to be seen if Samsung will integrate the soft-touch back we saw on the Galaxy Note 3 and the Galaxy Grand 2. Rumours also point towards an Android 4.3 with TouchWiz-ed. No further information is available with regards to specifications.
Samsung has in the past released multiple variants of the same device, so we can expect these rumours to turn out to be true. The Samsung Galaxy Grand Neo will most likely be in line with that of Galaxy Grand 2 in terms of design, though we can expect the device to be a bit cheaper than the Galaxy Grand 2. The device could hit the market sometime in early 2014.
