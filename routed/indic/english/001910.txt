Two days after the body of a five-year-old girl was recovered from a corn field in a Khanna village, the police Monday arrested a 49-year-old man, a native of Bihar who worked as a labourer at the same village.
 Police said that Lal Babu Rai, 49, a native of Amwa Kalan in Shibhar district of Bihar, had been living in the village for nearly a decade and used to cook food for the migrant labourers. He used to live in a rented rooms on the motor premises of a farmer where the girl was living with her father.
 Khanna SP (investigation) Pragya Jain said that on May 11, the accused tried to molest the minor girl and when she raised an alarm, he got angry, grabbed her by the neck and hit her several times, due to which she died on the spot. She added that the accused hid the body in the maize fields.
 Police added sections 363, 364 and 302 of the IPC and Section 12 of the POCSO Act in an FIR which was earlier registered at Khanna Sadar police station when the girl had gone missing.
 Sources said that the autopsy report of the minor indicated that she had deep injuries to her head, neck and shoulder. However, the external examination had not indicated rape or sexual assault and the vaginal swabs were further sent for detection of spermatozoa.
 Her father is a migrant labourer. The girl used to live with her father in a rented room on the premises owned by a farmer. Her mother had died due to cancer when she was just three months old, said the police.
