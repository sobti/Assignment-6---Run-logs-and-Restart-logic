The cultivated area of the state is 9.85 million ha comprising 51.4% of the total geographical area. The net irrigated area is 2.38 million ha constituting 24% of the total cultivated area. The red and black soils are deficient in nitrogen, phosphorus and zinc. The important crops are rice, fingermillet (ragi), sorghum, pearlmillet, pigeonpea, groundnut, sunflower and safflower. The major constraints for increasing agriculture production in the area are large dependence on rainfall and low productivity of the existing rice-rice system.
