Former India head coach Ravi Shastri recently compared captaincy of Virat Kohli and Rohit Sharma. Having worked with both of them, Shastri knows about their leadership qualities. Rohit Sharma recently replaced Virat Kohli as a skipper in ODIs. The selectors went with the 34-year old after Virat had stepped down from India’s T20I captaincy.
Speaking to star sports, Shastri compared the duo to Kapil Dev and Sunil Gavaskar.
“Their captaincy styles remind me of Sunil Gavaskar and Kapil Dev. Rohit is like Gavaskar – calculated, extremely efficient, calm, and composed. Virat is more like Kapil Dev – spontaneous, instinctive, goes with the gut feeling,” he said.
“I don’t think the team really worried too much about what was on the outside. They were professionals”, he further added.
