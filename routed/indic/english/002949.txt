 By Samriddhi Srivastava: Rakul Preet Singh has the sweetest birthday wish for boyfriend Jackky Bhagnani on his birthday. Jackky turned a year older today, December 25. Rakul took to Instagram to pen a heartfelt note to mark the occasion. The couple announced being in a relationship in October.
 On the occasion of Rakul Preet Singh’s 31st birthday, the couple made their relationship official. Today, December 25, on Jackky Bhagnani’s birthday, the actress wrote a sweet post for him. “Happy happy bdayy my sunshine. may you always keep smiling and spreading smiles the way you do! You know I wish you all that you desire. #happybday @jackkybhagnani (sic),” Rakul Preet Singh wrote along with a photo of Jackky.
 Rakul Preet is an actress who began her career in Tamil and the Telugu film industry. She slowly ventured into the Hindi film industry and is now doing back-to-back projects. She has Sidharth Malhotra-starrer Thank God, Production 41 with Akshay Kumar, Ayushmann Khurrana's Doctor G, John Abraham's Attack and Ajay Devgn's MayDay in her kitty.
 Jackky Bhagnani is an actor, producer and entrepreneur. He is the son of veteran producer Vashu Bhagnani. He made his debut as an actor in Kal Kissne Dekha in 2009 and went on to do many films in Bollywood.
