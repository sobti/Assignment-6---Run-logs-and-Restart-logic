 By Kumar Kunal, Arvind Ojha: Delhi woke up to this winter’s coldest morning on Sunday as the mercury dipped to 4. 6 degrees Celsius, according to the Indian Meteorological Department.
 The Safdarjung observatory recorded lowest minimum temperature of 4. 6 degrees Celsius on Sunday, signalling another ‘cold day’ in the national capital. The maximum temperature will be around 18 degrees Celsius, five notches below the normal.
 According to the IMD, when the minimum temperature is less than or equal to 10 degrees Celsius and the maximum temperature is at least 4. 5 degrees Celsius below the normal, it is said to be a 'cold day'.
 Cold wave-like conditions, brought on by chilly northwesterly winds, will continue to prevail in the city for the next few days.
 Air Quality Index (AQI) improved in Delhi but remained in the poor category. According to the System of Air Quality and Weather Forecasting And Research (SAFAR) India, the overall AQI in Delhi presently stands at 290 as against 319 recorded on Saturday.
 Meanwhile, the northern plains reported the lowest minimum temperature of this winter on Sunday. At 8:30 am, Churu and Ganganagar in Rajasthan recorded a low of -2. 6 degrees Celsius and 3 degrees Celsius respectively. In Haryana’s Narnaul and Hissar, the mercury plunged to season’s lowest of 1. 2 degrees Celsius and 2 degrees Celsius respectively.
