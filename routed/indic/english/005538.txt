New Delhi: In Delhi’s Wazirabad area, a woman had an illegal relationship with her husband’s friend. After this, the woman along with her lover killed her husband for becoming a hindrance in the illegal relationship.
 The accused After this the dead body was burnt. The Crime Branch of Delhi Police has arrested the friend of the deceased after disclosing this murder case on Thursday.
 According to the media reports, on the morning of January 2, the dead body of a completely burnt youth was found near Surghat. Meanwhile, 23-year-old Noorjahan (name changed) reached the police station and informed about her husband’s disappearance. When the police took Noorjahan to the spot, she identified the husband as Rashid Ali from the shoes of the dead body.
 After this, the police registered a case of murder and started investigating the matter. The team formed under the supervision of DCP Vichitra Vir Singh investigated the matter. Inspector Amit Tiwari and SI Kuldeep searched the CCTV footage and checked the CDR.
 During this, it was learned that Rashid was last seen with Munisuddin on the night of January 1. The location of the phones of Munisaduddin and Rashid was also near Surghat. On this basis, the Crime Branch interrogated Munisuddin after taking him into custody and he spilled all the secrets.
 Accused Munisuddin told that he works as a plumber in buildings, while Rashid was an electrician. For two years, he used to visit Rashid’s house.
 During this, he had an illicit relationship with Rashid’s wife Noorjahan. Since Rashid used to get work because of Munisuddin, he did not say anything to him, but often used to beat Noorjahan. He changed the house thinking that might lessen his frequent visits. The accused told that for this reason Noorjahan had planned to remove her husband from the way.
