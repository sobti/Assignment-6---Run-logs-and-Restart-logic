Balakrishna's landmark flick 'Gautamiputra Satakarni' completed Censor formalities Today. Censor Board has passed the movie without a single cut and offered it an U/A Certificate.
 Post the screening, Censor Members appreciated Team Satakarni for going the extra mile to remind Telugus about the forgotten history. 'War Sequence are going to be a major attraction in 'Gautamiputra Satakarni'. Krish showcased the Salivahana Era beautifully. Not just Balayya Fans, Every Telugu Movie Buff would love this movie,' as per Censor Buzz.
 The positive buzz around 'Gautamiputra Satakarni' is only getting bigger and better with each passing day. Looks like, This Balayya-starrer is going to be a sure shot winner during this Sankranthi season. Wait for the final verdict till the early hours of January 12th, 2017.
