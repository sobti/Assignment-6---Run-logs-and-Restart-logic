Pakistan pacer Shaheen Afridi was presented with a special souvenir by his teammates in the dressing room after completing 100 wickets in Test cricket. The pacer achieved the feat in the ongoing game against Sri Lanka at Galle.
Shaheen Afridi displayed another special performance as he made the new ball talk and troubled the Sri Lanka batters on the Day of the first Test of the series. The left-arm pacer bowled impeccable lines and lengths and put his team in a commanding position.
Shaheen Afridi ripped through Sri Lanka’s top order and took three wickets in quick succession of time to leave them tottering at 53/4. He completed his 100 Test wickets and after the day’s play, he was given a memento by his team in the dressing room. Mohammad Rizwan presented him with the souvenir and called him an inspiration for the team.

Shaheen Afridi became the 19th Pakistani player to achieve the feat. He also is the fourth quickest among fast bowlers from his country to the 100-wicket milestone.
Sri Lanka won the toss and opted to bat but the decision didn’t prove to be worthy as Shaheen Afridi’s sensational spell saw them tumbling. However, after that, Angelo Mathews (64) and Dhananjaya de Silva (94*) led the rescue charge and bailed their side out of trouble.
After the wicket of Mathews, Sadeera Samarawickrama (36) supported De Silva well but lost his wicket during the fag end of the day. At Stumps on Day 1, Sri Lanka were 242/6. For Pakistan, apart from Shaheen Afridi, Naseem Shah, Abrar Ahmed, and Agha Salman picked one wicket each.
