Every one Every Day Halifax is throwing a street party and you’re invited! Join the neighbourhood for music, dancing, games, crafts, and so much more. Gottingen St. will be shut down from Cornwallis to Cunard and there are lots of hands-on activities planned for you and your family. Bring your kids to have fun with jumbo games, jump rope, bubbles, chalk, and more! Don’t forget to bring your dancing shoes!
