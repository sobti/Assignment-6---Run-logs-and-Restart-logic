 Mumbai: Finding an affordable restaurant near Mumbai's Bandra-Kurla Complex (BKC) is a tiring task, despite it being one of the busiest and biggest commercial hubs in India's financial capital. However, now people here have a good news. The Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority (MMRDA) has decided to open a food court in the Urban Plaza to offer affordable meals.
 The food court will be coming up in the G block of BKC, located at the junction of Bandra-Kurla Link Road (BKLR) and the Asian Hart Hospital.
 The facility, which will also have retail shops and supermarkets, is expected to benefit a large number of people including employees at BKC and others who reach the location for various purposes.
 Many of the eateries currently functioning at BKC are unaffordable for a large number of people and some of them are extremely expensive. People often go to faraway locations like Kurla or Kala Nagar for affordable menu options or to depend on aggregators like Swiggy and Zomato.
 Situated in the suburbs of Bandra and Kurla, BKC is a prominent business district and an important financial centre located in Mumbai. It is and is spread across around 400 acres of land and houses several corporate offices, government offices, banks, and financial institutions. Some of the prominent companies with offices in BKC include the National Stock Exchange, Reliance Industries, Bharat Diamond Bourse, Citibank, Bank of America, and many more.
 Meanwhile, the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) is planning to open 24x7 food trucks at different locations in the city. The tender process for the food truck process has been started. As many as 50 moving eateries will be deployed in seven zones under the civic body.
