In this Ninaithadhai Mudippavan film, M. G. Ramachandran, Manjula Vijayakumar played the primary leads.
The Ninaithadhai Mudippavan was released in theaters on 09 May 1975.
Movies like Demonte Colony 2, Vidaamuyarchi, Thangalaan and others in a similar vein had the same genre but quite different stories.
The movie Ninaithadhai Mudippavan belonged to the Drama, genre.
