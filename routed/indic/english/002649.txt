The Gujarati New Year falls on Wednesday, October 26, 2022. People should keep in mind that the Gujarati New Year (shubh muhurat) runs from 4:18 p.m. (25 October) to 2:42 p.m. (October 26).
Every year, the Gujarati New Year brings delight for many as people make the most of this auspicious occasion to start new ventures or to take important decisions. People seek the blessings of Lord Krishna for their loved ones on this day. This day people also wish Varshabhinandan or Nutan Gujarati New Year to everybody.

FAQs to understand this occasion better:
- Why is the Gujarati New Year celebrated?
The festival is celebrated to end old accounts and begin new ones and is followed by Chopda Pujan.
- How is the Gujarati New Year different?
According to the Indian Lunar Calendar, the start of the year begins with Kartik month, and in Gujarat the New Year happens on the first bright day of Kartik (Ekam).
