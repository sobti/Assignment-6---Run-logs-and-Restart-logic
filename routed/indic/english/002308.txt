Businessman and IPL Franchise Kings X1 Punjab co-owner Ness Wadia submitted the list of 9 witnesses present at the Wankhede Stadium on May 30th in connection with the abuse and molestation case filed against him by former girlfriend Preity Zinta. The list include 3 men and 6 women.
 "We believe these people who were present would be able to provide independent and truthful facts about the events that took place at the stadium that evening, with regard to the false complaint filed by Preity Zinta against me," said Ness Wadia in his statement.
 Mumbai cops will record the statement of Ness Wadia and the witnesses he has named this week.
 Earlier, Preity Zinta has submitted the list of 14 witnesses who were present during the time of incident at Wankhede Stadium for the IPL match between Kings X1 Punjab and Chennai Super Kings.
