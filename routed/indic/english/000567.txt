Psychedelic mushrooms, commonly known as “magic mushrooms,” are a type of fungus that contains a naturally-occurring psychoactive compound called psilocybin. Psilocybin is a potent hallucinogen that can induce intense trips and alter the user’s perception of reality for several hours. Since ancient times, humans have been consuming psychedelic mushrooms for their profound effects on human consciousness, creativity, and spiritual experiences. Today, millions of people still use magic mushrooms for recreational, therapeutic, and spiritual purposes. In this article, we will explore what to watch under psychedelic mushrooms and give you some recommendations for enhancing your tripping experience.
What to Watch Under Psychedelic Mushrooms?
Watching movies or TV shows while under magic mushroom is a fantastic way to enhance your psychedelic experience. Choosing the right movie or show can amplify the emotions, visuals, and thoughts of your trip and take you on a surreal journey that can be life-changing. Here are some recommendations for what to watch under psychedelic mushrooms:
1. Alice in Wonderland (1951)
Alice in Wonderland is a classic animated fantasy movie based on the novel by Lewis Carroll. This whimsical movie follows Alice as she falls through a rabbit hole and explores a bizarre, dream-like world full of strange creatures, illogical events, and nonsensical dialogue. Watching Alice in Wonderland under magic mushrooms can intensify the surrealism, humor, and melancholy of the story and make you feel like you’re experiencing a dream or a nightmare. It’s a great movie choice for those who want to dive into their subconscious and explore their inner fears, desires, and thoughts.

Filmed over five years in twenty-five countries, this documentary shows the beauty and complexity of human cultures, religions, and traditions, as well as the environmental destruction and social injustices that threaten our planet. Watching Samsara under magic mushrooms can evoke strong emotions, such as awe, empathy, and sadness, and give you a sense of interconnectedness with all things. It’s an excellent movie for those who seek a profound and spiritual experience.
Fantasia is a timeless animated musical film that features eight visual and musical segments accompanied by the classical music of Bach, Tchaikovsky, and Beethoven, among others. Watching Fantasia under magic mushrooms can awaken your senses, imagination, and creativity and make you feel like you’re part of a cosmic symphony. 4. Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas (1998)

Filmed over five years in more than two hundred locations around the world, this series uses high-definition cameras and cutting-edge techniques to capture the wildlife, landscapes, and phenomena of the planet. Watching Planet Earth under magic mushrooms can deepen your appreciation, respect, and awe of the natural world and make you feel like you’re part of a mystical ecosystem. Q: Are psychedelic mushrooms safe?
A: Psychedelic mushrooms can be safe if consumed responsibly, in a safe environment, and with the right dosage. However, magic mushrooms can also be dangerous if misused, abused, or consumed by people with underlying mental or physical health conditions. It’s essential to research the effects, risks, and legal status of psychedelic mushrooms before consuming them.
Q: How long does a mushroom trip last?
A: The duration of a mushroom trip depends on various factors, such as the potency of the mushrooms, the dosage, the individual’s metabolism, and the environment. Typically, the effects of a magic mushroom trip can last from four to six hours, with the peak occurring between two to three hours after consumption.
Q: What are the benefits of using psychedelic mushrooms?
A: Psychedelic mushrooms have been shown to have various potential benefits, such as reducing anxiety and depression, increasing empathy and social connectivity, enhancing creativity and problem-solving, and inducing mystical and spiritual experiences. However, more research is needed to identify the long-term effects and risks of psychedelic mushrooms.
Q: Can I watch any movie or show under psychedelic mushrooms?
A: No. Choosing the right movie or show can enhance your tripping experience, but choosing the wrong one can lead to an unpleasant or overwhelming trip. It’s essential to choose movies or shows that align with your mood, intention, and sensitivity level. Avoid movies or shows with strong violence, disturbing content, or negative emotions.
Watching movies or TV shows while under psychedelic mushrooms can be an exciting and transformative experience. By choosing the right movie or show, you can enhance your trip’s emotions, thoughts, and visuals and explore your consciousness, creativity, and spirituality. Alice in Wonderland, Samsara, Fantasia, Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas, and Planet Earth are some great movie suggestions, but there are many other fantastic movies and shows that can enhance your tripping experience. Remember to consume psychedelic mushrooms responsibly, in a safe environment, and with the right dosage, and always do your research beforehand. Happy tripping!
