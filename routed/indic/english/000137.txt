Mumbai: An appalling case of medical negligence was reported in Chembur, Mumbai. A newborn baby was left with a 2 cm needle in his hip joint after a regular vaccination session at a Panvel clinic. It was only after 19 days of vaccination the parents took a note when the affected area began swelling up and the baby had recurrent fever.
 The child was born on June 7 and on June 10, the first vaccination session was conducted at the Panvel Nursing Home. On June 29 the child’s parents brought the kid to hospital because of recurrent fever where he was treated for osteomyelitis - bone swelling caused by an infection, in hip joint. Till now, there was no suspicion of a separate entity which could have caused an infection. The fever was still recurrent and the baby was referred to Bai Jerbai Wadia hospital, where CT scan showed presence of a 2 cm needle in baby’s hip joint.
 After the diagnosis, the parents revealed that the baby was given an intramuscular vaccine when he was three-day-old. According to a report by the Times of India, “The boy was lucky that it did not harm him in a big way. The parents told us that the baby would cry but, they understandably did not have any way to know why he was crying,” said the paediatrician surgeon. The needle was removed through a surgery, multiple X-rays were conducted to determine the exact place of the needle.
