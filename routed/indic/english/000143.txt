Megha Dhade's entry has brought a huge twist in this season of Salman Khan's Bigg Boss. The show has failed to create an impact unlike last year but Megha's entry boosted our hopes. She has been the winner of season 1 of Bigg Boss Marathi and her entry promised some sparks for the audience. Well, it is slowly turning out to be true. Being a winner she knows how to stay strong through the tasks and how to play the mind game to bend rules in her favour. But this is not turning out to be easy for her. This week's captaincy task is running and we can see how everyone is trying their best to convince the three contestants, Somi Khan, Megha Dhade and Deepak Thakur to leave the train. Yesterday, Somi gave up the task and now, Deepak and Megha are fighting against each other. But this fight is going to be a treat to watch.
All the members of the house will target Megha and will torture her so that she leaves the task. The video shared by the makers shows how Rohit Suchanti and Deepak will bring chilly juice for Megha and when she will refuse it, they will make sure she drinks it. The clip shows Megha suffering pain after drinking it but she is adamant to not quit.
After going through the clip, we wonder will Megha give up on the task or she will show how she emerged as the winner in the Marathi version. Today's episode of Bigg Boss 12 is surely going to give the drama that the audience has been eagerly waiting for.
