The Cat's Eye gemstone is known as Beryllium Aluminium Oxide in chemical terminology and is symbolised as Al2BeO4. The refractive index of the stone ranges between 1.744 - 1.755 and the hardness on the Mohs scale reaches close to 8.5 which makes it the third hardest natural stone known to exist. The density of the Cat's Eye gemstone is about 3.70 to 3.72. The chatoyancy effect in the stone is what defines its beauty and usage. This effect is caused in the gemstone due to an inclusion of very fine, parallel mineral fibers that reflect a beam of light in a single band like formation. The Cat's Eye gemstone or lehsunia stone usually flaunts a cabochon cut and is polished fittingly to give it a shiny and smooth surface. There are other gemstones too that manifest a Cat's Eye formation but Chrysoberyl Cat's Eye is known to be the best because of its strong, distinct and vivid effect. Hence, this gemstone enjoys the privilege of being called a 'Cat's Eye' by default, unless mentioned otherwise.

Certain physical properties of the Cat's Eye stone can help one buy an authentic gemstone and stay at bay from the spurious ones.
- Colour - The chatoyancy effect in the Cat's Eye gemstone is the indicator of its quality and genuineness. Chatoyancy effect is the thin strip of of light that runs across the gemstone from one end to another in a lengthwise fashion. This effect is known to be caused in the stone due to a hollow tube like inclusion or a needle inside it. On flashing a beam of light on the stone, it gives an impression similar to that of the closing and opening of the eye. The stone is available in a variety of colours ranging from yellow, greenish yellow, greyish green, brown to brownish yellow.
- Cut- The Cat's Eye gemstone or lehsunia stone needs to be cut in a careful and precise manner so as to clearly flaunt its chatoyancy effect. Hence, a very experienced and skilled lapidarist must handle this gemstone as it is of utmost importance that the stone clearly signifies the closing and opening of an eye when rotated under a beam of light. Also, the needles in the stone must be perpendicular to the stone to prove its authenticity. The most appropriate cut that is used to bring out the specifics of this gemstone is the cabochon cut which is a dome- like cut in round or oval shape. Post the cut, the stone is adequately polished to reveal its chatoyancy effect.
- Carat- Typically, the Cat's Eye is used in a size ranging from 0.10cts to 3.00cts. Though, gemstones of a bigger size can also be attained. Cat's Eye gemstones as massive as 150 carats also exist but are rare and expensive.
- Clarity- The Cat's Eye gemstone generally bears a milky appearance and the clear ones are the rarest of the rare.
