In a thrilling European encounter on Thursday evening, former Arsenal and Chelsea striker Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang etched his name in the highlight reels with a stunning performance. He capped off the match with a sensational bicycle kick that could well be the goal of the season.
The Stade Velodrome witnessed Aubameyang’s heroics as he swiftly restored Marseille’s lead just minutes into the second half, leaving the home fans in a frenzy.
With the game tied at 2-2, Aubameyang seized control, executing an acrobatic bicycle kick that outmaneuvered his marker Anton Gaaei, securing his second goal of the night and the third in a triumphant hat-trick.
Prior to this fixture, Aubameyang had been navigating a mini-goal drought, enduring five consecutive games without finding the net. However, on this crucial European night for Marseille, the seasoned striker showcased no signs of rust. Not only did he open the scoring from the spot, but he went on to add two more goals, including a late stoppage-time winner from 12 yards, completing a remarkable European hat-trick.
Aubameyang’s stellar performance against Ajax reignites hope for Marseille as they look ahead to their upcoming Ligue 1 clash against Rennes on Sunday evening.
The striker’s artistry was particularly evident in the goal that stole the show—a breathtaking overhead kick from inside the area, reminiscent of his previous exploits, including a similar goal for Arsenal in the Europa League against Olympiakos in 2020.
While Chuba Akpom netted a late consolation goal for Ajax, Marseille emerged victorious, and Aubameyang’s brilliance ensured they ascended to the top of their group. The third-minute stoppage-time penalty, securing his hat-trick, underscored Aubameyang’s pivotal role in Marseille’s triumph, leaving fans eagerly anticipating more magic in the ongoing European campaign.
Aubameyang’s potential goal of the season has not only revitalized his individual form but has also propelled Marseille to new heights in their European pursuits.
