We all know that, after directing Ram Charan, Srinu Vaitla is set to direct another Mega Hero. It's Varun Tej. This created a speculation whether Vaitla`s movie was cancelled or postponed?

My film with Srinu Vaitla garu will start rolling in Spain from 27th. " This should put to rest all of the naysayers' assumptions about Mister.
