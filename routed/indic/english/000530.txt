Dodda ayur Chhota Ladakh is place near kolar. Its just 60 kms from bangalore. Just one day ride and a must visit place with friends. Its a small abandonded area with no interaction with city noise. On weekends mostly people will visit but its less crowdy as compare to other weekend gateways.
Its an ideal location for bikers to plan short road trip and enjoy the ride. The highway NH75 is just smooth road with almost no patches and once you leave highway it directly comes to an offroad track which most of bikers enjoy.
