 In the first semifinal match of the ongoing ICC Women’s T20 World Cup in South Africa, Harmanpreet Kaur-led Team India will face five-time champion Australia at the Newlands Stadium in Cape Town. The Women in Blue finished second with three wins in four group stage matches, while the Southern Stars have moved closer to a record-extending sixth T20 World Cup title with a perfect record. Thursday’s clash will be a repeat of the 2020 T20 Women’s World Cup final, which was played at the iconic Melbourne Cricket Ground on March 8. The match between India and Australia is expected to be a cracker of a contest, as both teams have many star players in their ranks. Here are five players who are expected to play significant roles in the first knockout game.
 Smriti Mandhana: India’s vice captain and star opening batter Smriti Mandhana has been in red-hot form. In the last two matches, she has scored back-to-back fifties. She is the second-leading run scorer in the tournament despite missing the team’s first match against archrival Pakistan. The 26-year-old is expected to continue her ace form and deliver one more iconic performance for the team to seal a historic win over the defending champions.
 Richa Ghosh: Fresh from winning the ICC U-19 Women’s T20 World Cup, 19-year-old Richa Ghosh has impressed everyone with her power-packed performance. The right-handed wicketkeeper-batter has made vital contributions for India as a finisher in the first three matches and remained unbeaten. In the high-voltage match against Australia, she will again be a key player for Team India.
 Renuka Thakur: Star fast bowler Renuka Thakur registered the best bowling figures by an Indian in the T20 World Cup (men and women combined) when she got rid of five English batters for 15 runs last week. The 27-year-old is a big match player, and she would once again like to come up with her best show in probably the toughest match of her career to date.
 Alyssa Healy: Australian wicketkeeper and star opener Alyssa Healy has been among the runs in the ongoing tournament. In three matches, she has scored 146 runs so far. In the past, she has hurt India with her batting, and once again, she would like to do the same.
 Tahila McGrath: Even though in the ongoing ICC Women’s T20 World Cup, the world’s No. 1 T20I batter Tahila McGrath hasn't had much chance to bat, she is more than capable of making life difficult for any team single-handedly. In her last match against South Africa, she scored 57, and against India, she would like to add some runs to her tally.
