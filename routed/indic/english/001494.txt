It is a known fact that the industry heads from the film chamber had been going through a series of meetings regarding various issues that have been haunting the industry.
The core team headed by Dil Raju met the press to update the situation. In his speech, Dil Raju revealed that as producers they have decided to release Telugu films on OTT only after eight weeks after the theatrical release.
Dil Raju also added that a key decision has been taken regarding the ticket rates which will be revealed soon in the days to come.
Dil Raju also revealed that the core committee also understood as to where so much of money is being wasted in film production. But the key update was that no decision has been taken regarding the re-start of film shoots which have been stopped as of now.
Dil Raju finally ended by saying that in another five days, all the key decisions will be taken and an updated report about every issue will be announced officially.
