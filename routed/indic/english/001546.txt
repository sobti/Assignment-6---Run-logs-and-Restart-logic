Australia is gearing up for their first ICC World Cup 2023 match against India on October 8. In preparation for the tournament, the Australian team sought the assistance of Baroda off-spinner Mahesh Pithiya. He bears a striking resemblance in bowling action to the legendary Indian spinner, Ravichandran Ashwin.
During the last edition of the Border-Gavaskar Trophy, Pithiya had closely collaborated with the Australian team. They aimed to learn techniques to counter Ashwin. However, despite their efforts, Australia fell short in the Border-Gavaskar Trophy, with Ashwin emerging as the leading wicket-taker.
The Australian team management approached Pithiya to rejoin the squad before their crucial match against the hosts, India, on October 8. However, the 21-year-old spinner, after consulting with Baroda’s bowling coach S Aravind, declined the offer. Pithiya expressed his commitment to focusing on the domestic season rather than taking on additional commitments with the national team.
“It was undoubtedly an intriguing offer, but I am also a part of the Baroda team for the domestic season. It starts the following month. So I gave it some thinking. I talked to our coach, and told them I wouldn’t be able to attend the camp this time,” Pithiya told Sportstar.
Pithiya further revealed that he received the call to join the squad by October 4 in Chennai as soon as the BCCI announced Ashwin as the replacement for Axar Patel.
“I received the call as soon as the BCCI declared Ashwin as Axar’s replacement. Although working with foreign teams is always interesting, local cricket is my top priority. I have progressed so much since I have played for Baroda, therefore I decided to concentrate on improving my skills rather than join the Australian squad before a long season,” Pithiya concluded.
Australia’s first warm-up game against the Netherlands was called off due to rain. Despite the weather interference, Steve Smith and Cameron Green exhibited promising performances with the bat. Mitchell Starc delivered an exceptional hat-trick before rain interrupted play.
