M. P. Nagabhushan (96), noted advocate and a resident of Chamarajapuram, passed away on Oct. 11 at his residence in city.
 Son of late Siddappa, in whose name a road junction is named as Siddappa Square, he leaves behind two sons, two daughters and a host of relatives and friends.
 Last rites were performed at Veerashaiva Burial Grounds in Vidyaranyapuram here yesterday.
