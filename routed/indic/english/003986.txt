It’s been a long time since Sai Dharam Tej appeared on the big screen. Now, he is coming back with a new film, which has made the headlines again.
Sai Dharam Tej took to his social profiles and announced that the title and its glimpse of his next, tentatively called SDT 15, will be out on December 7, 2022. An intense poster has been shared to announce the same.
Samyuktha Menon is the female lead in this mystical thriller directed by Karthik Dandu. Sri Venkateswara Cine Chitra and Sukumar Writings joined hands to produce this flick, which has Anjaneesh Loknath’s music.
