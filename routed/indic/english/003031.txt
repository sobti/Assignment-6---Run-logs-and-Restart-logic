Puttur, Dec 26: President Fr Stany Pinto, campus director, St Philomena Campus quoted Mahatma Gandhi's words, "Seven Deadly Sins: Wealth without work, Pleasure without conscience, Science without humanity, Knowledge without character, Politics without principle, Commerce without morality, Worship without sacrifice. "
He said, "Jesus was named as Immanuel meaning God With Us. If God is with us then definitely we will take a complete form. "
The chief guest, Dr Antony Prakash Montheiro, principal, St Philomena College said, "Today, we have lost the sense of humanity and the sense of being real. Jesus , the son of God reminds us to develop humanity inorder to become a successful human beings. "
The guest of honour, Mamachan M, president, parent teacher association said, "Whenever virtue subsides and wickedness prevails, God takes birth to destroy evil and to save the world. "
Parent teacher association members were present on the occasion.
 Fr Ashok Rayan Crasta, principal, St Philomena PU college, Puttur welcomed the gathering. Sathyalatha Rai M, department of English proposed a vote of thanks. Suma P R, department of English compered the programme.
