KMC mayoral candidate Sirjana Singh casts her vote in Kathmandu.
 KATHMANDU: Mayoral candidate of Kathmandu Metropolitan City (KMC) Sirjana Singh has cast her vote.
 Singh, who represents the ruling coalition, cast her vote at the polling station at Nepal Yuvak Secondary School, Paknajol, KMC-16.
 She is also the leader of the Nepali Congress.
